Conceptual site plan: sloped, uncleared single-family lot

A sample of the layout I draft for a county pre-application (PreScreen) conceptual site plan: boundaries and setbacks, contours and slope, proposed residence with distances to each property line, wetland and buffer, septic and well, driveway and its grade, and the limit of clearing.

Please read: this is a sample on a fictional parcel and is not for submission. Contours, wetland, well and septic positions are invented to show the layout. On a real lot I draw strictly from the client's land survey, topo study, septic design and wetland report, and list anything missing as a question. Setbacks shown are assumed, not county rules. It is a drafted conceptual plan, not a stamped survey or an engineered design.

Download the sample sheet (PDF, A3)

What the real plan would be built from

  • Land survey: boundaries, easements, adjacent street and parcel number
  • Topo study: contours and slope, steep areas, driveway grade
  • Septic design: tank, drainfield and reserve area, well location and separations
  • Wetland report: wetland edge and the required buffer width
  • House footprint and sketches, plus the county's current pre-application checklist

What I would flag, not guess

  • Setbacks for the parcel's zone and any critical-area rules
  • Driveway grade against county access and fire standards
  • Limit of clearing and tree retention on an uncleared lot
  • Anything the surveyor, septic designer or wetland professional must confirm
